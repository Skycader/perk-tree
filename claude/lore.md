# Setting lore notes

## Amuons (амюоны) — souls are not a single entity

The soul in this setting is composed of **amuons**, the fundamental
particles of existence itself. Documented behavior (see
`configs/default/default.notes.js`):
amuons can partially transfer to a victorious Angel without "reheating"
the soul; a dead body's amuons, lacking their living catalyst, act
autonomously trying to reconstruct that person's identity from memory —
but this reconstruction is explicitly **not the person**; a body built by
amuons from scratch is a **clone**; some species (Аксантиане) live in
permanent symbiosis with amuons.

**Key epistemic point (stated directly by the user, treat as settled
lore)**: we know how amuons *behave* (their preferences, their rules of
engagement, how they respond to death/victory/symbiosis) — but what they
fundamentally *are* is unknown, the same way physicists know how a tau
lepton or muon behaves without knowing what it intrinsically is. This is a
deliberate, load-bearing analogy: amuons occupy the same epistemic status
in-universe as fundamental particles do in real physics — well
characterized behavior, opaque essence.

**Why this matters for future perk/lore work**: this is the mechanism that
lets the perk tree describe metaphysics (soul, memory, identity,
consciousness) with the same engineering-spec rigor as physics (mass,
kinetics, EM fields) — measured ranges, thresholds, degradation under
overload — without ever needing a full theory of what a soul *is*. It also
sidesteps the classic clone/teleporter identity paradox by construction:
since amuon nature is unanswerable, "is the clone the same person" simply
has no available answer in-setting — the lore only describes observable
outcomes (autonomous reconstruction ≠ the person; scratch-built body =
clone), never the metaphysical fact of the matter.

Any new perk, note, or lore text involving souls, memory transfer,
cloning, possession, or consciousness should stay consistent with:
1. Soul = a population of amuons, not one indivisible thing (and per
   the partial-transfer-to-an-Angel behavior, that population is
   apparently countable/divisible — a plausible basis for any future
   numeric "soul capacity/bandwidth" mechanics).
2. Amuon *nature* is and should remain unexplained — only their
   observable behavior is fair game to write mechanics around.
